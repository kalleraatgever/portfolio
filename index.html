Live brainstorm sessions

Stormrz

From brainstorm to roadmap. A group joins from their phones by scanning a QR code, no account needed. Every idea strikes the big screen as a storm cloud, and then everyone votes on the best ones.

  • Real-time sync between the big screen, a host panel and every phone, over WebSockets
  • Identical ideas merge into one bigger cloud, so popular themes stand out on their own
  • Four ways to vote: one vote, pick several, rank a top N or divide points, revealed on a 1-2-3 trophy stand
  • Presenter mode, co-host links, CSV and JSON export, and reusable session templates

Group trip app

cotage

Everything a group needs on a trip, in one installable app: who paid what, what's planned and who's winning. Built mobile first as a progressive web app, with live updates and phone notifications.

  • Expenses in any currency, split equally, by parts, percentages or amounts, with a settle-up plan
  • Group and personal chat with photos, GIFs and stickers, delivered live
  • A shared calendar for flights, plans and outings, with invitees and locations
  • Games: a drinks leaderboard, bets, knockout tournaments, house-rules scoring and votes
